Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Baby Kick Counter & Contractions

Your privacy is our priority. All data is stored locally on your device. No accounts. No servers. No tracking.

1. Data Collection

Baby Kick Counter & Contractions collects the following data that you manually enter:

  • Kick Count Data: Number of kicks, timestamps, session duration, and average intervals
  • Contraction Data: Contraction start/end times, duration, and intervals between contractions
  • Optional Notes: Any notes you choose to add to sessions
  • Settings: Your app preferences (due date, display units, haptic feedback, etc.)

We do NOT collect:

  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any automatic data collection

2. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device only.

  • Data is stored using SQLite database on your device
  • Data is never transmitted to servers
  • Data is never shared with third parties
  • No cloud backup (unless you use PRO backup feature, which stores data locally on your device)
  • No external access to your data

3. Data Usage

Your data is used solely for:

  • Tracking and displaying your kick counts and contractions
  • Generating session summaries and history
  • Calculating trends and intervals (PRO feature)

Your data is NEVER used for:

  • Advertising
  • Analytics
  • Third-party sharing
  • Marketing
  • Data selling

4. Data Security

  • All data is encrypted using device-level encryption
  • No external access to your data
  • You can delete all data at any time through app settings
  • No accounts means no data breaches possible

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete: You can delete all data at any time through app settings
  • Export: You can export your data as PDF or CSV (PRO feature)
  • Backup: You can create local backups (PRO feature)
  • No Account Required: No account means no data stored on servers

6. Third-Party Services

Baby Kick Counter & Contractions does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

The app uses:

  • Apple In-App Purchase: For PRO subscription (handled by Apple, we do not receive payment information)
  • Local Storage: SQLite database on your device

7. Children's Privacy

Baby Kick Counter & Contractions is designed for expectant mothers and their partners. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

9. Medical Disclaimer

Important: Baby Kick Counter & Contractions is designed as an informational tracking tool only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

The information and data collected in this app should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of information from this app.
